Beyond Borders, Beyond Graduation.

Every year, hundreds of thousands of international students travel to Australia to pursue higher education. What happens when they complete their degrees? An evidence-led inquiry into employment, salary parity, and post-study reality.

Audience: Australian Public & Policy Makers Scope: Higher Education & Graduate Labour Market Semester 2, 2026

PLACE MATTERS

Where Opportunity Is Concentrated

Outcomes are not evenly distributed across Australia. These maps connect the graduate story to place, showing where universities are located and how earnings vary between states and territories.

FIELD REALITIES & EXPERIENCE
