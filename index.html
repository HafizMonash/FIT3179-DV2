AUSTRALIAN GRADUATE OUTCOMES · 2025

After the degree.

A degree is a beginning, not a single destination. Explore how employment, salary and further study differ across fields — and place those outcomes in the wider Australian labour market.

DATA STORY QILT + ABS 2024–2026

THE QUESTION

What happens after university?

Graduate outcomes are often reduced to one number. But full-time employment, overall employment, salary and further study tell different parts of the story. This page compares outcomes across fields and years, then uses the broader labour market as context.

Important: the QILT figures are graduate outcomes. The ABS maps show the general state and territory labour market; they are not maps of graduate outcomes.

THE TAKEAWAY

Graduate outcomes are multidimensional. Employment, salary and further study reveal different aspects of the transition from university to work. The useful question is not simply which field is “best”, but which outcomes matter, for whom, and at what point after graduation.
